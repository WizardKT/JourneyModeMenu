# Journey Mode Menu

Ever wanted Terraria's Journey Mode duplication system in Minecraft 1.12.2?

Now you can legally destroy progression.

## Features
- Research items by sacrificing them
- Permanently remember researched items
- Retrieve unlocked items anytime
- Simple `/jm` command system
- Fully standalone mod
- Forge 1.12.2 support

## Current State
This mod is currently in early development.

Implemented:
- Item research system
- Saved researched items
- Item retrieval through commands

Planned:
- Full Journey Mode-style GUI
- Required item counts for research
- Better progression balance
- Mod compatibility improvements

## Inspiration
Inspired by Terraria Journey Mode and partially by ProjectE.

The concept is simple:
> give item  
> unlock item  
> become economically unstable

## Commands
/jm progress - Sends all your research progress
/jm consume - Consume all your items in hand to research
/jm give - Give 64 of full researched item for your choise
/jm research - <**Cheat**> Research item in hand for free
/jm remove - Remove item from researched for your choise
/jm clear - Clear all your researches
